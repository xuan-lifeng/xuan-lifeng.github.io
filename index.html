2026 to 2027 · Imperial College London

Anatomy Aware Surgical Robotics

MEng thesis supervised by Dr Stuart Bowyer. I am developing a stereo near infrared fluorescence pipeline for reconstructing three dimensional vascular geometry from paired images.

QuestionHow can vascular geometry be recovered robustly enough to support future anatomy aware navigation?
MethodsVessel segmentation, stereo correspondence, triangulation, depth estimation, centreline extraction, synthetic ground truth, and tissue mimicking phantoms.
EvaluationDepth and geometry accuracy, plus analysis of segmentation and correspondence uncertainty.
Computer visionStereo imagingNIRF3D reconstruction

Research output: Manuscript in preparation for December 2026 conference submission.

02

2025 to 2026 · Imperial College London

Dynamic Receptive Fields in Mouse Auditory Cortex

Comparative modelling of neural responses to natural ultrasonic vocalisations in 46 single units, supervised by Dr Andriy S. Kozlov.

QuestionCan a stimulus dependent neural model capture response properties missed by static receptive field methods?
MethodsFive layer causal Conv1D network in PyTorch, automatic differentiation through the Jacobian, STA, STC, MNE, and dynamic STRF analysis.
ResultdSTRF achieved median Pearson correlation of 0.339 on held out firing rate prediction and identified temporal hold in 43.5% of analysed neurons.
PyTorchConv1DNeural dataModel evaluation
