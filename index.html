02 / About me

Research background

EducationMEng Biomedical EngineeringImperial College London / 2023–2027Expected graduation: June 2027
Download CV

I’m a Biomedical Engineering student based in London, with interests in computational imaging, neural data modelling and clinically oriented systems engineering.

My current thesis, supervised by Dr Stuart Bowyer, explores stereo NIRF imaging of subsurface vessels. I previously led a collaborative neural-modelling project with Dr Andriy S. Kozlov, comparing static and dynamic receptive-field methods.

Alongside research, I have worked on wearable assistive communication, live patient-monitoring software and analogue biomedical instrumentation. The project pages distinguish my individual contributions from shared work and retain the limits of the reported measurements.
